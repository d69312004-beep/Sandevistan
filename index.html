User guide: gestures and options

Home

  • Tap a note to open it. Double-tap it for duplicate and delete buttons.
  • Press and hold a note to see its details.
  • Swipe a note right to pin or unpin it. Swipe left to delete it (you can undo).
  • Double-tap the top or bottom bar to hide it. Tap it once to bring it back.
  • Press and hold any button to see what it does.
  • The round letter button switches profiles. The ⋯ button has Settings, import, layout and sort.
  • Swipe down on a panel or tap outside a pop-up to close it.

Writing a note

  • Swipe right from the left edge to save and go back.
  • Double-tap empty space in the note for focus mode. Double-tap again to leave it.
  • Press and hold an attached picture to zoom in.
  • The bottom bar adds photos, handwriting, the assistant, fact checks and exports.

Handwriting page

  • One finger or a pen draws. Two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to move the page.
  • The move tool pans with one finger. The percentage button resets the view.
  • Tools: pen, highlighter, eraser, line, arrow, rectangle, circle, triangle (the drop turns on fill) and text (type first, then tap the page).
  • Select: drag a box around strokes, then drag to move them. Clear deletes the selection, or the whole page if nothing is selected.
  • Layers 1 to 3 stack your work. The eye hides the current layer.
  • Arrows at the top switch pages. Plus adds one and the cross removes one. Page setup changes paper, margins and color.
  • Double-tap the page to hide the tools and fit the page. Double-tap again to bring them back.
